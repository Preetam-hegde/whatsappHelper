# WhatsApp Web AI Assistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-30

This extension adds optional AI drafting tools to WhatsApp Web.

## Data processed

- When you use an AI action, the extension reads the recent visible chat text needed to draft a reply.
- Your API key, model preference, and settings are stored in Chrome extension storage on your device.
- When you request an AI feature, the selected chat context and your prompt are sent directly to the provider you selected: OpenRouter or Google AI Studio. Those providers process the request under their own policies and terms.
- The extension does not operate a developer server, sell user data, run advertising profiles, or collect analytics.

## Your choices

AI actions are optional. You can remove your API key from the popup or uninstall the extension. Do not use an AI action for messages or credentials you do not want to send to the selected AI provider.

## Limited use

Data is used only to provide the extension's described reply-drafting features. It is not sold, used for personalized advertising, or transferred for an unrelated purpose. The extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
